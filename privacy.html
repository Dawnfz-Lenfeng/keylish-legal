键语 Keylish 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 26 日

键语（Keylish，以下简称“本 App”）是一款中文拼音键盘，可在候选词下可选显示英文译文对照，用于被动学习。 本政策说明我们在你使用本 App 时如何处理信息。

我们收集的信息

我们不收集、不存储、也不向开发者服务器传输你键入的任何内容。这包括拼音串、候选词、上屏文字以及译文。 本 App 没有账号系统、没有广告，也不包含第三方分析或追踪 SDK。

键盘的“完全访问权限”（Full Access）

iOS 要求键盘扩展在开启“完全访问权限”后才能访问网络与共享容器。键语只在以下两种用途使用该权限：

  • 译文对照：向 iOS 请求 Apple 系统翻译服务，为当前候选词生成可选的英文译文。
  • 解锁状态共享：通过 App Group（group.com.keylish.keyboard）在键盘扩展与键语 App 之间共享译文对照的试用/解锁状态。

你的键入内容与译文不会被上传到开发者服务器。Apple 系统翻译服务由 Apple 提供并受 Apple 隐私政策约束。 即使不开启完全访问权限，中文与英文的直接输入功能仍然可用。

设备上的本地数据

  • Rime 输入方案与拼音词库、离线英汉词典随 App 一起打包，全部在设备本地运行。
  • 译文缓存与偏好设置保存在 App 沙盒及 App Group 容器中，不会离开你的设备。

App 内购买

译文对照提供免费试用和终身解锁两种 App 内购买项目，试用时长以 App 内购买页显示为准。付款由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理， 我们不接触也不存储你的支付信息。购买与解锁状态保存在设备本地，并可通过“恢复购买”向 Apple 查询。

第三方

本 App 使用 Apple 提供的系统能力：系统翻译服务与 App Store 内购。相关信息处理请参阅 Apple 隐私政策。

儿童隐私

本 App 不收集任何用户数据，适合所有年龄段使用。

数据保留与删除

我们没有任何服务器端数据。删除本 App 即会删除设备上与其相关的全部本地数据。

政策变更

如本政策发生变更，我们将在本页面更新并修改“最后更新”日期。

联系我们

如有疑问，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系我们。

Keylish Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Keylish is a Chinese pinyin keyboard that can optionally show English translations beneath candidates for passive learning. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit anything you type to the developer's servers. This includes pinyin input, candidates, committed text, and translations. The app has no accounts, no ads, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Keyboard "Full Access"

iOS requires a keyboard extension to enable Full Access before it can use the network and shared containers. Keylish uses this permission only for:

  • Translation comparison: requesting Apple's system translation service for the current candidate.
  • Unlock status sharing: sharing trial/unlock state between the keyboard extension and the Keylish app through App Group group.com.keylish.keyboard.

Your keystrokes and translations are never uploaded to the developer's servers. Apple's translation service is provided by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Chinese and English direct input remains available even without Full Access.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Rime schemas, the pinyin dictionary, and the offline English–Chinese dictionary ship with the app and run entirely on device.
  • Translation caches and preferences are stored in the app sandbox and App Group container and never leave your device.

In-App Purchases

Translation comparison offers a free trial and a one-time lifetime unlock; the trial length is shown on the in-app purchase screen. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store; we never receive or store your payment information. Purchase state is kept on device and can be restored from Apple at any time.

Third Parties

The app uses Apple system services: system translation and App Store in-app purchases. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles that data.

Children's Privacy

The app collects no user data and is suitable for all ages.

Data Retention and Deletion

We hold no server-side data. Deleting the app removes all related local data from your device.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date.
